# Baoyu Content Production

## Overview
Use this umbrella for the Baoyu family of Chinese knowledge visuals. Choose the content type, lock style and palette early, then maintain consistency across panels, sections, or illustration variants.

## When to Use
- 微信 or Chinese article illustrations.
- 知识漫画, educational comics, biography comics, or tutorial comics.
- 信息图, visual explainers, and structured infographic layouts.

## Content Types
### Article Illustration
Focus on one memorable metaphor for the article thesis. Keep palette and typographic tone consistent with the article.

### Knowledge Comic
Break the topic into scenes: hook, setup, confusion, explanation, payoff. Keep characters, palette, and line style stable.

### Infographic
Pick a layout family first: timeline, comparison, framework, flow, hierarchy, matrix, checklist, map, or dashboard. Use concise Chinese labels.

## Common Pitfalls
1. Mixing illustration, comic, and infographic goals in one artifact.
2. Changing style or palette across panels.
3. Using long paragraphs where labels should be short.

## Verification Checklist
- [ ] Content type explicit.
- [ ] Style and palette consistent.
- [ ] Chinese labels concise and legible.
